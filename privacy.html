Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Astro Outlaw is made by Jordan Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of our own. Your progress, Stardust and settings are stored only on your device.

Purchases

The Full Game unlock and Stardust packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and shown to other players under your Game Center name, as set in your device's Settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
