Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Astro Outlaw is made by Jordan Games. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

The game has no accounts, no ads and no servers of our own. Your progress, Stardust and settings are stored only on your device.

Play statistics

To see how the game is played and decide what to improve, the game sends anonymous play statistics through Unity Analytics, a service run by Unity Technologies. These include things like runs started and finished, sectors reached, upgrades and routes chosen, purchases made in the game, and how smoothly the game runs. Unity also records basic device information (such as the device model, operating system and language), an approximate country or region worked out from your connection, and a random identifier created for this install. We don't collect your name, email or contacts, and we don't use this data for advertising.

You can turn play statistics off at any time in the game's Settings: set SHARE STATS to OFF. To ask for statistics already collected from your device to be deleted, email us. Unity's handling of this data is described in Unity's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Full Game unlock and Stardust packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple and shown to other players under your Game Center name, as set in your device's Settings. Apple's privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The play statistics described above don't identify a player, and they can be switched off in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
