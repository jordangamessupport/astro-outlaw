Astro Outlaw

A roguelite space shooter for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email jordangames.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version if something isn't working.

How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to fly; your ship fires on its own. Clear each sector, pick an upgrade, and choose your route across the star map. Beat the Dreadnought at the top of the map to reach the next galaxy. Stardust you collect is yours to keep: spend it in the Hangar on new engines, weapons, shields, hulls and specials.

FAQ

What does the Full Game unlock include?

Galaxy 1 is free. The one-time Full Game purchase unlocks galaxies 2 and beyond, the other enemy fleets, more ship parts, more specials and the Daily Challenge.

I bought the Full Game. How do I get it back on a new device?

Open the Full Game screen and tap RESTORE, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Stardust packs are used up when bought, so they can't be restored.

What is the Daily Challenge?

One galaxy, the same map and the same loadout for everyone that day, with unlimited tries. Your best score goes to the Daily Challenge leaderboard. A new one starts every day at midnight UTC. It's part of the Full Game.

How do leaderboards and achievements work?

They use Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings, then tap GAME CENTER on the Stats screen to see them.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If you delete the app, your progress, Stardust and settings are deleted with it.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Yes, in Settings on the title screen (the gear in the top corner).
